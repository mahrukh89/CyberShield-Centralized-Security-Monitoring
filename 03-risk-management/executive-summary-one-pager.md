# Executive Summary — CyberShield Centralized Security Monitoring

**One-page read for recruiters and hiring managers.**

---

**The problem:** Security telemetry was scattered across endpoints, identity, cloud, and network systems — causing fragmented visibility, slow investigations, and inconsistent escalation.

**The project:** A 12-week, hybrid-delivery initiative to centralize priority telemetry, stand up detection and alert-escalation workflows, build management reporting, and formally hand over an operational monitoring capability.

**The result:** Closed on schedule, 9 of 9 success criteria met, zero critical defects at closure, one controlled change request, Sponsor acceptance recorded.

| KPI | Target | Result |
|---|---:|---:|
| Priority sources onboarded | ≥90% | 92% |
| Detection use cases validated | ≥90% | 95% |
| UAT / Training completion | 100% | 100% / 100% |
| Critical defects at closure | 0 | 0 |

## What This Demonstrates

- **Full lifecycle ownership** — charter → requirements → planning → risk → execution → monitoring → change control → closure
- **Governance discipline** — RACI, stakeholder engagement strategy, weekly governance cadence, documented decisions
- **Risk fluency** — probability × impact scoring, active RAID management, one critical risk mitigated without schedule slip
- **Change control** — one CR raised, impact-assessed, and baselined without scope creep
- **Technical literacy** — solution architecture understood well enough to sequence a realistic WBS and hold technical conversations with engineering (`08-architecture/solution-architecture.md`)
- **Agile-hybrid delivery** — predictive governance layered with 4 sprint cycles
