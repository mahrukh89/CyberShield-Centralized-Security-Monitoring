# Communication Management Plan

## Communication Objectives

- Provide timely project visibility.
- Ensure decisions are documented.
- Surface risks and blockers early.
- Maintain stakeholder alignment.
- Create an auditable communication rhythm.

| Audience | Information | Frequency | Channel | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsor | Executive status, risks, decisions | Weekly | Meeting / Email | PM |
| Core Team | Tasks, blockers, dependencies | 2x weekly | Teams / Jira | PM |
| Security Lead | Technical progress | Weekly | Technical Review | PM |
| SOC | Testing and readiness | Weekly | Workshop | Security Lead |
| IT Operations | Infrastructure dependencies | Weekly | Meeting | PM |
| Compliance | Controls/documentation | Biweekly | Review | PM |
| Project Team | Sprint progress | Weekly | Jira / Stand-up | PM |

## Escalation Rules

- Critical blocker: escalate within 4 business hours.
- High risk: escalate within 1 business day.
- Scope-impacting request: initiate change request.
- Schedule variance >10%: recovery-plan review.
- Budget variance >10%: sponsor review.
